Postal-code boundaries: Statistics Canada 2021 forward sortation area (FSA) boundary file, simplified for speed. Rural codes (T0A, T0B, T0C…) are split into their full postal codes, one area per community, drawn around each code's GeoNames location. Outer edges follow StatCan; the lines between full codes inside a rural FSA are approximate, and some codes centre on their delivery area rather than the town itself.
